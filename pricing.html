Pricing FAQ

When do I get charged?
Your 7-day free trial starts immediately, no credit card required. You'll only be asked for payment information if you decide to continue after the trial. If you choose the annual plan, you're billed once per year. Monthly plans are billed on the same date each month.
How do refunds work?
All paid plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within 30 days of your first payment, email us at contact@thedogcoach.co with the subject "Refund Request" and we'll refund 100% — no questions asked. See our Refund Policy for details.
Can I switch from monthly to annual?
Yes. You can upgrade from monthly to annual at any time from your account dashboard. We'll credit your unused monthly days toward the annual plan, so you never pay twice for the same period.
