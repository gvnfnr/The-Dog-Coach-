Pricing FAQ

When do I get charged?
Your plan starts immediately upon purchase. The Behavioral Reset is billed as a one-time 6-month payment of €79. The Full Access Annual plan is billed once per year at €149. All payments are secure and encrypted.
How do refunds work?
All plans include a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within 14 days of purchase, contact us at support@thedogcoach.co and we'll process your refund with no questions asked.
What's the difference between the two plans?
The Behavioral Reset (€79) gives you 6 months of full access with a custom behavioral profile, AI chat support, and progress tracking — perfect for solving specific issues. The Full Access Annual (€149) includes everything plus lifetime plan updates, priority AI chat, and exclusive masterclasses for ongoing excellence.
Can I switch plans?
Yes! You can upgrade from the Behavioral Reset to Full Access Annual at any time. Contact our support team and we'll apply a prorated credit toward your upgrade.
