The Method

The Science Behind It

The Dog Coach is built on the gold standard of modern canine behavioral science: positive reinforcement combined with systematic desensitization. Unlike traditional obedience training that focuses on commands, we address the root cause of problem behaviors — almost always rooted in anxiety, unmet needs, or miscommunication.

Our protocols are developed with certified canine behaviorists and reviewed against peer-reviewed research in applied animal behavior. Every training sequence is force-free — we never recommend punishment, corrections, or aversive tools.

The AI layer personalizes at a scale no single trainer could match. By analyzing patterns across 50,000+ successful cases, the model predicts which intervention works best for your specific dog — and adjusts in real time based on your daily check-ins.
