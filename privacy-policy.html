Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · GDPR Compliant

1. Who We Are

The Dog Coach is operated by Discover Group Sàrl, a company registered in Geneva, Switzerland. We are the data controller for information collected through our platform at thedogcoach.co. If you have any data-related questions, contact us at contact@thedogcoach.co.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data when you use our Service:

  • Account data: Email address, name, and password (hashed)
  • Dog profile data: Dog's name, breed, age, behavior issues, and training responses you provide during the quiz and daily sessions
  • Usage analytics: Pages visited, features used, session duration, and in-app interactions (collected via privacy-respecting analytics)
  • Payment data: Billing information processed by Stripe — we never store full card details on our servers
  • Communications: Messages you send via our AI chat and email correspondence with our team

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and personalize the coaching service to your dog's specific profile
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send transactional emails (receipts, account updates, training reminders)
  • Improve our AI models using aggregated, anonymized behavioral data
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes on other platforms.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to provide the Service you've subscribed to
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Analytics and service improvement
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Marketing emails (you can withdraw consent at any time)
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Tax records and financial compliance

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except for data we're required to retain by law (e.g., financial records for 10 years under Swiss law). Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@thedogcoach.co. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We use analytics cookies to understand how users interact with our platform — these are privacy-respecting and do not track you across other websites. You can manage cookie preferences via your browser settings. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Stripe: Payment processing (Stripe's Privacy Policy applies to payment data)
  • OpenAI API: Powers our AI coaching chat (conversation data is processed by OpenAI under strict data agreements)
  • Vercel: Hosting infrastructure (EU data processing available)

Each of these providers has agreed to data processing agreements consistent with GDPR requirements.

9. International Transfers

Your data is primarily processed within the European Economic Area. Where data is transferred outside the EEA (e.g., to OpenAI servers in the US), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission to ensure adequate protection.

10. Contact & Data Protection

For any privacy-related requests or questions, contact our Data Protection contact:
Discover Group Sàrl
Email: contact@thedogcoach.co
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
