Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🛡
30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We're so confident in The Dog Coach that we offer a full refund if you're not satisfied within 30 days of your purchase. No questions asked.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you purchase any paid plan and are not satisfied with the results within the first 30 days, we will refund 100% of your payment. This applies to both monthly and annual plans. We believe in the program — and we want you to feel completely safe trying it.

You do not need to "prove" that you followed the program or provide any documentation. We trust our customers. Simply send us a refund request and we'll process it promptly.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, send an email to:

contact@thedogcoach.co

Use the subject line: "Refund Request"

Include your account email address in the message. You don't need to explain why — though we'd love to hear your feedback if you're willing to share.

3. Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days of receiving your request. The refund will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 3–5 business days for the funds to appear in your account.

4. Annual Plan Refund Specifics

Annual plan subscribers are covered by the same 30-day money-back guarantee as monthly subscribers. If you request a refund within 30 days of your annual subscription payment, you will receive a full refund for the entire annual amount — not just one month.

If you subscribed to an annual plan, cancelled before the end of the year, and it's been more than 30 days since your payment, we do not offer pro-rated refunds for unused months.

5. What's Not Refundable

  • Requests made more than 30 days after the original payment date
  • Free trial periods (no charge, so no refund applicable)
  • Renewals where a refund was already issued for a previous billing period

6. Chargebacks Policy

We ask that customers contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with their bank or credit card issuer. Chargebacks create significant administrative burden for small businesses and result in additional fees. If you're unhappy with the Service, please reach out to us first — we will resolve the issue quickly and fairly. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in account termination.

Have questions? Email us at contact@thedogcoach.co — we typically respond within one business day.
